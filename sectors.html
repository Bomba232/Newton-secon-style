Sector Knowledge. Cross-Border Perspective.

Newton works across sectors where international markets, technology, investment and strategic relationships intersect.

Healthcare & MedTech

Healthcare innovation requires access to complex networks of clinical, commercial and institutional stakeholders. Newton supports international healthcare and MedTech companies seeking new markets, partnerships and commercial opportunities.

Market-entry assessment · Hospitals and healthcare providers · Clinical partners and KOLs · Distributors · Pilot and validation sites · Research institutions · Investors and strategic partners

Technology & AI

We work with technology businesses seeking customers, partners, investors and international expansion opportunities. Particular emphasis is placed on understanding the practical application of the technology and identifying organisations with a genuine reason to adopt it.

Agriculture & Food

Newton supports opportunities across agriculture, food, animal health and agricultural technology. Our network can include producers, technology providers, distributors, integrators, agricultural organisations and investors.

Energy & Natural Resources

International energy and natural-resource projects frequently require access to specialised investors, strategic partners and international industry networks. Newton supports selected opportunities through targeted market research, investor identification and strategic introductions.

Financial Services

We work with technology providers and businesses operating across financial services, investment and international trade. Our work focuses particularly on identifying commercial users, strategic partners and international market opportunities.

Consumer & Retail

Retail is undergoing rapid technological change. Newton supports innovative technology companies seeking relationships with retailers, distributors, technology integrators and other commercial partners.
