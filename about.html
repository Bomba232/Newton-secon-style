Newton Incorporation is an international business development and strategic advisory company headquartered in Hong Kong. We help companies, investors and entrepreneurs identify and develop opportunities across markets, industries and borders.

Main message

Business Development That Goes Beyond Advice.

International expansion often begins with research.

But research alone does not create a market.

Companies need to know which organisations to approach, who makes the decisions, how to reach them and how to turn initial conversations into commercial relationships.

This is where Newton operates.

We combine market intelligence with practical business development.

Our work can include market assessment, opportunity identification, competitor and partner mapping, decision-maker research, targeted outreach, introductions and ongoing support in developing commercial relationships.
